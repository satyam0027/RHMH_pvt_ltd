How to Pick the Right Agency

Start with your goal. If you want long-term organic traffic, choose an SEO-strong agency. If you need quick leads, look for Google Ads expertise. If you need a new website too, go for a full-service team.

Ask for case studies, a clear reporting format (for example, access to Google Search Console data) and a contract without lock-ins. Check client reviews on platforms like Clutch. Be careful with anyone who guarantees a #1 ranking.

If you're a local business, make sure the agency also optimizes your Google Business Profile and publishes helpful, people-first content.
