What are PPC Services?

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) advertising is a digital marketing model where advertisers pay only when users click on their ads.

It allows businesses to:

  • Reach targeted audiences instantly
  • Generate leads quickly
  • Track performance in real-time

Modern PPC includes search ads, display ads, video ads, and remarketing campaigns.
